Questions

We do not have a warehouse yet. Can we still use it?

Yes. Most startups we talk to only have their app database and logs. During the pilot we build the raw tables from your backend on AWS or GCP, then the aggregates and semantic layer on top.

What counts as a governed metric?

A metric with one definition, an owner, tests, and a known date range and grain. Everyone sees the same list, and the agent answers from it.

Do we need a data engineer?

Not a full team. Someone on your side owns the systems that feed your data. For anything bigger, our experts help per task.

What happens after the pilot?

If you continue on Startup or Scale, the full $20,000 is credited to your first year. If you stop, you keep the tables and definitions we built.
