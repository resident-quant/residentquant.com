How did revenue grow in 2014, and what drove it?
Q
✓ Metric revenue from agg_sessions_daily, by month

Revenue grew from $393K in 2013 to $1.08M in 2014, 2.7 times. Three things multiplied:

20132014Change
Visits112,781233,422×2.07
Conversion rate6.60%7.22%×1.09
Revenue per order$52.81$63.80×1.21
Revenue$393,248$1,075,612×2.74

Traffic did most of it, and paid nonbrand search alone added $424K. Revenue per order rose because of cross-sells. The Hudson River Mini Bear launched in February 2014 and earned $101K as a second item in the cart.

View SQL
SELECT year(session_date)            AS year,
       SUM(sessions)                 AS visits,
       SUM(orders) / SUM(sessions)   AS conversion_rate,
       SUM(revenue) / SUM(orders)    AS revenue_per_order,
       SUM(revenue)                  AS revenue
FROM agg_sessions_daily
WHERE year(session_date) IN (2013, 2014)
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1;
